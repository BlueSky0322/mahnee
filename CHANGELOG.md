# Changelog

What changed for the people using the app, newest first, by the month it reached `main`.
Every pull request that changes what a person sees or can do (`feat`, `fix`, `perf`,
`refactor`) adds one line here in its own branch, under the current month and the kind
of change: Added, Changed, Fixed or Removed. Write it in plain words, as the pull
request's Summary would put it, with no file names. The conventions check enforces it.

## 2026-11

### Added

- The app itself: sign in, see who you're signed in as, switch between light and dark,
  and install it on your phone or computer. Accounts and spending come next.
- The repository's working rules: how branches, commits and pull requests are named and
  written, checked on every commit, push and pull request.
